Privacy Policy

Issued by SolteQ Innovations Ltd for MindPilot & Digital Services
Last Updated: September 1, 2026 • Legal Entity: SolteQ Innovations Ltd (Kubwa, Abuja, Nigeria)

Welcome to SolteQ Innovations Ltd ("SolteQ," "we," "our," "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy, safeguarding personal data, and maintaining strict transparency. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the MindPilot mobile application and all hosted software, APIs, and web services operated by SolteQ Innovations Ltd.

By downloading, installing, or using MindPilot or any SolteQ digital product, you acknowledge and agree to the data collection and processing practices described herein.

1. Information We Collect

To provide high-performance features including cognitive journaling, local offline storage, AI-driven reflection analysis, and custom reminder alarms, we collect the following limited categories of information:

  • Authentication Credentials: When you register or sign in, authentication is securely processed via Google Firebase Authentication (email addresses, display names, and unique cryptographic user IDs).
  • User Content & Journaling: Your journal entries, streak milestones, wellness metrics, and encrypted chat messages are stored locally on your device and synchronized via Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Push Notification Tokens: We collect Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens to route your server-side AI reflections and local reminder notifications accurately.
  • Diagnostic & Device Diagnostics: Network connectivity state is monitored locally on-device to enforce offline data preservation and prevent data collision during carrier disruptions.

🔒 Offline-First Privacy: While your device is offline, your journaling records and personal entries remain exclusively inside your device's secured local cache. No data is transmitted to the cloud until a validated secure connection is established.

2. How We Use Your Information

SolteQ Innovations Ltd uses collected information solely to power, secure, and refine product functionality:

  • To synchronize journal entries, progress milestones, and streak achievements across authorized devices.
  • To execute secure server-side AI reflection routines via Firebase Cloud Functions at intervals configured by the user (1–3 hours).
  • To trigger local looping reminder alarms and scheduled focus checkpoints.
  • To maintain security audits, invalidate revoked sessions upon manual logout, and protect account integrity.

3. Data Storage & Security Standards

Security is fundamental to our software engineering principles at SolteQ:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: All synchronized data is hosted on enterprise-grade Google Firebase and Google Cloud server infrastructure protected by multi-layered encryption at rest and in transit (SSL/TLS).
  • Session Revocation: When logging out of MindPilot, your active authentication session is immediately invalidated across our database gateways to prevent physical device intrusion.

4. Third-Party Services

We work exclusively with vetted infrastructure providers who adhere to rigorous global security standards:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: Powers database storage, authentication, push alerts, and serverless compute.
  • Google Analytics: Collects anonymized telemetry to assess stability and performance.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full sovereignty over your data:

  • Insight Preferences: You can configure or disable AI insight intervals anytime via MindPilot App Settings.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account, database records, and Firestore documents at any time by contacting our support team below.

6. Regulatory Compliance

This policy complies with applicable global and regional data protection frameworks, including the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Google Play Developer Policies, and Apple App Store Review Guidelines.
